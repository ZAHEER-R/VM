About Us

A family-run chemist serving Machilipatnam and nearby villages since 1989.

Our story

Vijetha Medical & General Stores was founded by Rachakula Lakshmaiah and has operated from Koneru Centre, Azad Road, Machilipatnam since 1989. For over three decades the shop has been a reliable source of medicines, hospital and surgical supplies, veterinary products and everyday wellness items for the town and the villages around it.

The business continues as a family concern under Rachakula Venkateswarlu (Proprietor) and Rachakula Srinivas (Sub-Proprietor). Customers know the shop both as Vijetha Medical and by the long-standing local name Nowroji Medicals.

Whether you visit in person, send a list or prescription on WhatsApp, or call, the team confirms every order and can deliver to your door in Machilipatnam and nearby villages.
